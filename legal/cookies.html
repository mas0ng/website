Scope

This notice covers cookies, local storage, session storage, scripts, tags, and other technologies that store information on or access information from a browser or device when mas0ng.com is used.

The current public pages do not use these technologies for behavioural advertising, cross-site tracking, or third-party advertising analytics.

Public-page preferences

When you choose to dismiss an informational privacy notice, the site stores a small local preference so that the same notice does not immediately appear again. The current values are mas0ng_privacy_notice_acknowledged_v1 on mas0ng.com and mas0ng-bio-privacy-notice-v1 on bio.mas0ng.com.

These values record only a user-interface choice. They are limited to the origin that set them and remain until cleared or replaced.

Sign-in cookies and security storage

The private sign-in service uses storage that is necessary to authenticate users, protect sessions, and prevent abuse. The current authentication cookie is mas0ng_session (or a configured equivalent), which is normally HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax, and expires after seven days or sooner on logout, revocation, or security invalidation.

mas0ng_session_bootstrap is a short-lived HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax cookie used to make a newly issued session available safely across mas0ng.com subdomains. It expires after about 90 seconds. Server-side login challenges normally expire after five minutes, QR login records after two minutes, and failed-attempt rate-limit records after 15 minutes.

Public pages may send an existing session cookie to the authentication service to check whether the browser is already signed in. An anonymous status check does not create a session.

Cloudflare Turnstile

Cloudflare Turnstile is loaded only when a feature needs a security challenge, such as sign-in or the private security-report form. It may store or access information and process browser, device, network, and challenge signals to distinguish legitimate requests from automated abuse.

Turnstile is used for security rather than advertising. Its own technology and retention are governed by Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Tools and protected apps

Some tools store information locally when you request a feature, such as timer state, temporary console output, interface preferences, music preferences, caches, or draft state. Session storage normally ends with the browser session. Local storage remains until the feature replaces it or you clear it.

Local browser state stays on the device unless the relevant feature explains that it will be uploaded, synchronised, or saved to an account.

When consent or an exception is used

UK rules generally require clear information and consent before storing or accessing information on a user's device unless a legal exception applies. Authentication, fraud prevention, security, and selections a user asks the service to remember may qualify for an exception where the legal conditions are met.

UK law also permits certain low-risk statistical or appearance and functionality uses without consent where the applicable conditions, including clear information and a simple free way to object, are met. mas0ng.com does not currently use that route for public-page analytics.

If non-exempt advertising, tracking, or other optional storage is introduced, it will not be activated before the required choice is obtained.

Your controls

You can clear or block cookies, local storage, and session storage using browser settings. To remove only the current privacy-notice preference, use show the mas0ng.com notice again or show the bio.mas0ng.com notice again.

Blocking storage that is necessary for authentication or security may prevent protected services from working. Public pages should remain available.

Changes and contact

This notice is reviewed when browser storage, security services, or site features change. Questions about cookies or device storage can be sent to mason@mas0ng.com. Personal-data information is available in the Privacy Policy.
