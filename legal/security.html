Public static-site issues go to a private GitHub advisory. Authentication, API, Worker, and private-infrastructure issues use the private report form.

Authorised access

Protected pages and features are available only to authorised users with a valid session. Access may be refused, challenged, rate-limited, suspended, or revoked where authentication fails or a request appears unsafe, abusive, unauthorised, or inconsistent with the Terms of Use.

Security controls and Filestore

Security controls can include session validation, per-user authorisation, origin checks, encrypted sensitive requests, path confinement, rate limiting, and challenge verification. Filestore browsing requires an authorised site session, while create, upload, rename, and delete actions may also require a separate short-lived Media0 server-control session.

No description on this page is a guarantee that a particular control is infallible or a disclosure of every internal security measure.

Security information and monitoring

Sign-in and protected requests may record IP address, timestamp, user agent, requested page, authentication outcome, challenge result, failed-login reason, rate-limit event, and related security information. This is used to authenticate users, detect abuse, investigate faults, and protect private services.

Limited alerts about successful, failed, or blocked sign-in attempts may be sent through a third-party notification service. The Privacy Policy identifies relevant recipients, retention, rights, and the data protection complaint process.

Automated security checks

Automated checks may validate sessions, apply rate limits, require a challenge, or allow or block a request based on authentication and security signals. They are used to protect private areas rather than to profile ordinary public visitors or make legal or similarly significant decisions about them.

Cloudflare Turnstile

The sign-in page and private security-report form may load Cloudflare Turnstile when a security challenge is needed. Turnstile processes browser and request signals to help distinguish legitimate use from automated abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Responsible security research

Security research must stay within systems, accounts, and data that you are authorised to test. This page does not grant permission to access another person's account or data, bypass access controls on private infrastructure, disrupt service, or carry out activity that would otherwise be unlawful.

  • Use the smallest proof needed to demonstrate the issue and stop once the issue is confirmed.
  • Do not download, retain, alter, or disclose data that is not yours.
  • Do not use denial-of-service, destructive testing, phishing, social engineering, credential stuffing, malware, or high-volume automated scanning.
  • Do not test third-party systems merely because they are linked to or used by mas0ng.com.
  • Report the issue promptly and allow a reasonable opportunity to investigate before public disclosure.

Reporting a vulnerability

Use the reporting flow above this policy. A vulnerability limited to the public static website can be reported through the website repository's private GitHub security advisory. Concerns involving authentication, sessions, authorisation, protected applications, APIs, Workers, or private infrastructure should use this page's private report form.

Include the affected service, impact, smallest reliable reproduction steps, and a redacted request or proof of concept where useful. Do not include live passwords, session tokens, private keys, or unrelated personal data. You can also email mason@mas0ng.com.

Handling reports and disclosure

Reports are reviewed according to severity, reproducibility, affected systems, and available resources. No fixed remediation time or bug bounty is promised unless a separate written programme says otherwise.

Good-faith reports that follow this policy are treated as security reports rather than as permission to publish confidential information or continue testing after you have been asked to stop. Nothing on this page can grant immunity from laws or third-party rights that mas0ng.com does not control.

Your responsibilities as an authorised user

  • Keep login details, one-time codes, server-control codes, and sessions private.
  • Sign out on shared devices and revoke or clear sessions if a device is lost or no longer trusted.
  • Do not disable or bypass authentication, challenges, rate limits, or access controls.
  • Report unexpected sign-in, session, upload, AI, or protected-app activity promptly.

Contact

Security reports can use the reporting flow on this page. General security questions can be sent to mason@mas0ng.com. Personal-data concerns and complaints are handled under the Privacy Policy.
