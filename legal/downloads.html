The sender can set an expiry time, a maximum number of downloads, or both. A link stops allowing new downloads when either applicable limit is reached. Its download allowance is shared between all recipients of that link.

A download counts when the service reserves it to begin the transfer, rather than when your browser finishes saving it. An interrupted or failed transfer can therefore use an allowance, and allowances are not automatically restored. Restricted public downloads do not support pausing and resuming a transfer. If you need another attempt, reopen the original link and complete verification again; the link must still be available and have an allowance remaining.
