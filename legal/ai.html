Scope and UK audience

This policy applies to AI features operated through mas0ng.com. The service is operated from the United Kingdom and is directed to people in the UK. It is not marketed as a service for users outside the UK, although technical access from another country may still be possible.

The Privacy Policy explains the wider handling of personal data. This AI Policy adds information that is specific to prompts, model providers, uploaded files, generated content, and AI-related storage.

What is processed

When an authorised user uses an AI feature, mas0ng.com may process the prompt, relevant conversation history, selected model and settings, filenames, attachment content, extracted text, generated images or other generated content, model responses, timestamps, and usage or error information needed to provide and protect the feature.

Only submit material that you are entitled to use and process. Do not assume that an AI conversation is an appropriate place for confidential information merely because the feature is access-controlled.

AI providers and international processing

Prompts and relevant context are sent to the provider selected for the request, currently OpenAI or DeepSeek. OpenAI may also process supported files for text extraction as described below.

These providers may process information outside the UK under the provider terms, privacy information, account settings, and data-processing arrangements that apply to the service being used. Where mas0ng.com is responsible for a restricted international transfer, the transfer must use a mechanism permitted by UK data protection law.

Uploaded files and text extraction

Readable text files may be processed directly to provide attachment context. Images and PDF files may be sent to OpenAI to extract visible text, even when another model provider is selected for the later conversation. Extracted text may then be included in a request to the selected provider.

A file may still be stored and shown as attached when its contents cannot be extracted. Do not upload passwords, private keys, authentication secrets, unnecessary personal data, or third-party confidential material.

Storage and retention

Chats and files may be saved so that an authorised user can reopen them. Deleting a chat removes its saved chat and attached files from mas0ng.com, subject to temporary infrastructure or backup copies that may persist until they rotate.

By default, files are retained for pinned chats and the five most recent unpinned chats. Files from older unpinned chats may be deleted while chat text remains. Temporary chats are not intentionally saved by mas0ng.com after the tab is closed. AI providers may retain their own service records under the applicable provider settings and terms.

Lawful basis and data rights

Where personal data is involved, processing is based mainly on legitimate interests in providing, maintaining, and securing the requested private feature. Contract may apply where a feature is expressly supplied under a contract, and legal obligation may apply where information must be retained or disclosed by law. Consent is used only where a specific processing activity requires it.

Information about individual rights, recipients, international transfers, retention, and how to make a data protection complaint is in the Privacy Policy.

Accuracy, decisions, and professional use

AI output can be inaccurate, incomplete, biased, outdated, or unsuitable. Important output should be independently checked before it is relied on. AI output is not a substitute for legal, medical, financial, security, or other regulated professional advice.

mas0ng.com does not use AI output to make solely automated decisions about public visitors or authorised users that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

Restricted content and user responsibilities

  • Do not submit passwords, API keys, authentication codes, private keys, or other secrets.
  • Do not submit special-category, criminal-offence, financial, identity, or health information unless the feature expressly supports it and there is a lawful and necessary reason.
  • Do not submit another person's personal data or confidential material without a lawful basis and any permission that is required.
  • Do not request or use output for unlawful, abusive, exploitative, rights-infringing, or security-bypassing purposes.
  • Review generated content before publishing or acting on it and make sure your use does not infringe intellectual-property, privacy, confidentiality, or other rights.

Children and age-appropriate use

Protected AI features are not intended for children under 13. A person under 18 should use a protected AI feature only where access has been specifically authorised and the feature is appropriate for that user. If an online feature is likely to be accessed by children, additional UK child-data safeguards may apply irrespective of the site's intended audience.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when models, providers, storage rules, or legal requirements change. Material changes will be reflected in the revision date and, where appropriate, brought to authorised users' attention.

Questions, AI data requests, or concerns can be sent to mason@mas0ng.com.
